1. สเปกฮาร์ดแวร์ระดับอุตสาหกรรม (Hardware Stack)
• Main Processing Unit: ESP32 Microcontroller 240MHz Dual-Core (Wi-Fi/BT Built-in)
• Drive Controller: L298N Dual H-Bridge Heavy-Duty Motor Driver
• Actuator & Spraying: 5V Optocoupler Solid-State Relay + Mini Diaphragm Pump
• Power Management: Dual-Cell 18650 Li-ion (7.4V) + Buck Converter Step-Down 5V Constant
